Privacy Policy for Mboa-Zen

Last Updated: October 19, 2026

Mboa-Zen ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by Mboa-Zen.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide to us: If you create an account, we may collect your phone number to authenticate you.

Health and Wellness Data: We store the data you voluntarily log, such as water intake, meals, workouts, and check-in responses. Currently, this data is stored locally on your device (via local storage).

Usage and Analytics Data: We collect anonymous, aggregated data about how you interact with the app (e.g., which screens you visit, if you complete onboarding) to improve the app experience.

Error and Crash Data: We use a third-party service called Sentry to monitor crashes. If the app crashes, Sentry may collect device information, operating system version, and the state of the app at the time of the crash.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Mboa-Zen app.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our app.
  • Understand and analyze how you use our app to fix bugs and enhance features.

3. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party service providers to monitor and analyze the use of our app. Currently, we use:

  • Sentry: For error tracking and crash reporting. You can review Sentry's privacy policy at https://sentry.io/privacy/.

4. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
